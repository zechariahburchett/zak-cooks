## Prep Details

* **Prep time:** 15 mins
* **Cook time:** 50 mins
* **Servings:** 4 servings
* **Method:** Stovetop
* **Cuisine:** American

---

## Ingredients

* 1 lb ground beef
* ½ cup crushed cornflakes
* 2 tbsp water
* 1 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
* ½ tsp chopped onion
* 1 egg, beaten
* 2 tbsp ketchup
* ½ tsp sage
* Dash pepper
* 1 can tomato soup
* 1 can water (for sauce)
* Flour (for coating patties)

---

## Instructions

1. **Form the patties:** Combine all ingredients except the tomato soup, 1 can of water, and flour. Mix well. Form the mixture into patties.
2. **Brown patties:** Roll the patties in flour and brown on both sides in a skillet over medium heat.
3. **Simmer in gravy:** Mix 1 can of water with the tomato soup and pour over the browned patties. Reduce heat to low and simmer for 45 minutes.
