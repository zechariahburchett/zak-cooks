## Prep Details

* **Prep time:** 15 mins
* **Cook time:** 30 mins
* **Servings:** 6 servings
* **Method:** Stovetop
* **Cuisine:** American

---

## Ingredients

* 1 lb ground beef (85/15)
* 1 large, sweet onion (diced)
* 2 cans chili mild beans (Brooks or similar)
* 1 can whole kernel corn (drained)
* 1 28oz can diced tomatoes
* 2 1/2 tsp chili powder
* 2 tsp cumin
* Dash of cinnamon (to taste)

### Optional Garnishes
* Sour cream
* Cheddar cheese
* Saltines

---

## Instructions

1. **Brown beef and onion:** Over medium heat, cook ground beef and diced sweet onion in a large pot until the meat is browned and the onion is tender. Drain off excess fat.
2. **Combine ingredients:** Stir in the chili mild beans, drained corn, diced tomatoes, chili powder, cumin, and dash of cinnamon.
3. **Simmer:** Bring the mixture to a boil. Reduce heat to low, and simmer for 15 minutes, stirring occasionally to let the spices blend.
4. **Serve:** Ladle into bowls and serve hot with your favorite toppings!

---

## Serving Options

Top with sour cream, a sprinkle of cheddar cheese, and a side of crisp saltines for crushing into the bowl.
