## Prep Details

* **Prep time:** 15 mins
* **Cook time:** N/A (Chill time: 1–2 hours)
* **Servings:** 8 servings
* **Method:** Refrigerate
* **Cuisine:** American

---

## Ingredients

* 8 oz cream cheese, softened
* 2 tsp mayonnaise
* 4 oz dried beef, finely chopped (divided)
* 1 tsp yellow mustard
* 1 tsp dried minced onion

---

## Instructions

1. **Soften cream cheese:** Soften cream cheese in the microwave.
2. **Prep dried beef:** Finely chop the dried beef into small pieces. Reserve ¼ of the chopped beef in a small dish for coating.
3. **Combine ingredients:** In a large mixing bowl, combine the softened cream cheese, mayonnaise, yellow mustard, dried minced onion, and the remaining ¾ of the chopped dried beef. Mix thoroughly until smooth.
4. **Form the ball:** Use your hands to shape the mixture into a firm, tight ball.
5. **Coat the outside:** Roll the cheese ball in the reserved dried beef until the entire surface is coated.
6. **Refrigerate:** Wrap tightly in plastic wrap and refrigerate for at least 1–2 hours before serving to allow the flavors to meld and the cheese ball to firm up.

---

## Serving Options

Serve chilled alongside sturdy butter crackers (like Ritz or Town House), bagel chips, or fresh pretzel snaps.
