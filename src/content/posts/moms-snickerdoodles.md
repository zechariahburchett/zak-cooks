## Prep Details

* **Prep time:** 15 mins (Chill time: 1 hour)
* **Cook time:** 6–8 mins
* **Servings:** 5 dozen (2-inch cookies)
* **Method:** Bake
* **Cuisine:** American

---

## Ingredients

### Cookie Dough
* 1 cup shortening
* 1 ½ cups white sugar
* 2 eggs
* 2 ¾ cups sifted flour
* 2 tsp baking powder
* ½ tsp salt

### Cinnamon-Sugar Coating
* 2 tbsp sugar
* 2 tsp cinnamon

---

## Instructions

1. **Cream wet ingredients:** Cream the shortening, white sugar, and eggs together until thoroughly combined and smooth.
2. **Add dry ingredients:** Add the sifted flour, baking powder, and salt. Mix thoroughly to form a dough.
3. **Chill:** Chill the dough in the refrigerator for at least 1 hour.
4. **Prep oven & coating:** Preheat oven to 400°F. In a small bowl, mix together 2 tbsp sugar and 2 tsp cinnamon for the coating.
5. **Shape & roll:** Roll chilled dough into balls approximately the size of walnuts, then roll each ball in the cinnamon-sugar mixture until coated.
6. **Bake:** Place on baking sheets and bake at 400°F for 6 to 8 minutes, until lightly brown around the edges but still soft in the center. Allow to cool before serving!
