## Prep Details

* **Prep time:** 10 mins
* **Cook time:** N/A (Chill time: overnight)
* **Servings:** 4–6 servings
* **Method:** Refrigerate
* **Cuisine:** American

---

## Ingredients

* 1 cup sugar
* ½ cup vinegar
* ½ cup water
* Salt, to taste
* 2 Cucumbers, sliced
* 1 Sweet Onion, sliced

---

## Instructions

1. **Make the brine:** In a medium bowl or jar, mix together the sugar, vinegar, water, and salt until well combined and the sugar dissolves.
2. **Add vegetables:** Add the cucumber slices and onion slices to the mixture, ensuring they are fully submerged in the brine.
3. **Refrigerate:** Cover and refrigerate at least overnight before serving.

---

## Serving Options

Serve chilled as a crisp side, on sandwiches, or straight out of the jar!
