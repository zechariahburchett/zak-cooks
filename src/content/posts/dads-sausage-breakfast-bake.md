## Prep Details

* **Prep time:** 15 mins
* **Cook time:** 30 mins
* **Servings:** 6–8 servings
* **Method:** Bake
* **Cuisine:** American

---

## Ingredients

* 12 oz sausage links
* 2 tbsp water
* 1 can mushroom soup
* 2 eggs
* ¾ cup milk
* 2 tbsp oil
* 1 cup flour
* 1 cup cornmeal
* ¼ cup sugar
* 1 tbsp baking powder
* Margarine or butter (for greasing the baking dish)

### Optional Toppings
* Honey
* Maple syrup
* Fruit preserves

---

## Instructions

1. **Prep oven & dish:** Preheat oven to 350°F. Grease a 12x8-inch baking dish with margarine or butter.
2. **Cook sausage:** In a covered skillet over medium heat, cook the sausage links in 2 tbsp of water for 5 minutes. Uncover and continue cooking until the sausages are browned. Drain well and set aside.
3. **Mix wet ingredients:** In a bowl, combine the mushroom soup and eggs, mixing well. Gradually stir in the milk and oil until smooth.
4. **Mix dry ingredients & combine:** In a separate bowl, combine the flour, cornmeal, sugar, and baking powder. Add the soup mixture, stirring just until moistened (do not overmix).
5. **Assemble & bake:** Pour the batter into the greased baking dish. Arrange the cooked sausage links on top of the batter. Bake at 350°F for 30 minutes, or until the bread springs back when lightly touched.

---

## Serving Options

Serve warm with honey, maple syrup, or your favorite fruit preserves drizzled on top!
