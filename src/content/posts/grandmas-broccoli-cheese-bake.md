## Prep Details

* **Prep time:** 15 mins
* **Cook time:** 30 mins
* **Servings:** 6–8 servings
* **Method:** Bake
* **Cuisine:** American

---

## Ingredients

* 2 (10 oz) packages frozen chopped broccoli
* 1 can mushroom soup
* 2 cups grated cheese (Velveeta)
* 1 med onion, chopped
* 1 clove garlic, minced
* 1 small onion, grated
* ¼ tsp salt
* ⅛ tsp pepper
* 2 cups breadcrumbs
* 2 tbsp sage
* ¼ cup oleo (margarine or butter)
* Water (a few tablespoons, as needed)
* Paprika (for garnish)

---

## Instructions

1. **Prep oven & broccoli:** Preheat oven to 325°F. Cook the frozen chopped broccoli according to package directions and drain well.
2. **Mix base filling:** In a bowl, combine the cooked broccoli, mushroom soup, 1 cup of the grated cheese, chopped medium onion, minced garlic, salt, and pepper. Move the mixture into a 12x7½x2-inch baking dish.
3. **Make crumb topping:** In a separate small bowl, mix the breadcrumbs, sage, small grated onion, and melted oleo. Add just enough water to moisten the crumb mixture.
4. **Assemble:** Spread the breadcrumb mixture evenly over the casserole. Top with the remaining 1 cup of grated cheese and sprinkle with paprika.
5. **Bake:** Bake at 325°F for 30 minutes, or until the cheese is bubbly and lightly browned on top. Serve warm!
