## Prep Details

* **Prep time:** 5 mins
* **Cook time:** 15 mins
* **Servings:** 4 servings
* **Method:** Stovetop
* **Cuisine:** American

---

## Ingredients

* 1 lb hamburger (ground beef)
* 1 can Campbell's Condensed 25% Less Sodium Cream of Mushroom Soup
* 1 large sweet onion (diced)
* Hamburger buns (for serving)

---

## Instructions

1. **Prepare onions:** Wash and dice the sweet onion.
2. **Brown hamburger:** In a skillet over medium heat, add the diced onion and raw hamburger. Cook until hamburger is browned. Drain off excess fat.
3. **Add soup & simmer:** Add 1 can of Campbell's cream of mushroom soup and stir to combine.
4. **Serve:** Spoon warm onto hamburger buns and enjoy! Great with a slice of american cheese or just how they are!
