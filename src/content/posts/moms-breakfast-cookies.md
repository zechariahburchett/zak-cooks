## Prep Details

* **Prep time:** 15 mins
* **Cook time:** 15–18 mins
* **Servings:** 2 dozen cookies
* **Method:** Bake
* **Cuisine:** American

---

## Ingredients

* ½ cup softened butter
* ¾ cup sugar
* 1 egg
* 1 cup flour
* 2 cups CornFlakes
* ½ cup raisins
* 10 strips bacon (cooked and crumbled)

---

## Instructions

1. **Cream wet ingredients & flour:** Cream the softened butter and sugar together. Beat in the egg, then mix in the flour until well combined.
2. **Add mix-ins:** Stir in the crumbled bacon, CornFlakes, and raisins until evenly distributed.
3. **Drop on baking sheet:** Drop rounded tablespoons of dough 2 inches apart onto an ungreased baking sheet.
4. **Bake:** Bake at 350°F for 15 to 18 minutes, or until lightly brown. Allow to cool slightly before serving!
