## Prep Details

* **Prep time:** 15 mins
* **Cook time:** 40 mins
* **Servings:** 6 servings
* **Method:** Stovetop
* **Cuisine:** American

---

## Ingredients

* 1 lb ground beef
* 1 cup chopped onion
* 1 cup chopped green pepper
* 2 cloves garlic, minced
* 2 cans tomato soup
* 1 can whole kernel corn (with liquid)
* 2 tbsp chili powder
* 1 tbsp vinegar

### Optional Garnishes
* Shredded cheese
* Sour cream
* Chopped green onions
* Corn chips

---

## Instructions

1. **Brown beef and vegetables:** Over medium heat, cook ground beef, chopped onion, green pepper, and minced garlic in a pot until the meat is browned and the vegetables are tender. Drain and pour off the excess fat.
2. **Combine remaining ingredients:** Stir in tomato soup, corn (including its liquid), chili powder, and vinegar.
3. **Simmer:** Bring the mixture to a boil. Reduce heat to low and simmer uncovered for 30 minutes, stirring occasionally.
4. **Serve:** Ladle into bowls and serve warm with your choice of garnishes!

---

## Serving Options

Top with shredded cheese, sour cream, sliced green onions, and a crunch of corn chips.
