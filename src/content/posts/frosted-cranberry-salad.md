## Prep Details

* **Prep time:** 20 mins
* **Cook time:** N/A (Chill time: 4+ hours or overnight)
* **Servings:** 8–10 servings
* **Method:** Refrigerate
* **Cuisine:** American

---

## Ingredients

* 2 boxes cherry Jell-O
* 1 cup finely chopped celery
* 1 (15 oz) can crushed pineapple, drained (reserve juice)
* 1 can cranberry sauce (jellied)
* ⅓ cup lemon juice
* 1 cup lemon-lime pop (7Up)
* 8 oz whipped topping (Cool Whip)
* 8 oz cream cheese, softened
* Pecans (chopped, for garnish if desired)

---

## Instructions

1. **Prepare juice liquid:** Drain the crushed pineapple into a measuring cup, keeping the juice. Add enough water to the pineapple juice to equal 1 cup of liquid.
2. **Dissolve gelatin:** Bring the 1 cup of juice/water mixture to a boil in a small saucepan. Add the 2 boxes of cherry Jell-O gelatin and stir until completely dissolved.
3. **Blend cranberry mixture:** Place the jellied cranberry sauce, lemon juice, and dissolved gelatin liquid into a blender (or use a hand mixer in a bowl) and blend until smooth.
4. **Mix & set:** Combine the blended cranberry mixture with the 1 cup of lemon-lime pop. Stir in the drained crushed pineapple and finely chopped celery. Pour into a dish and refrigerate until fully set.
5. **Make frosting & assemble:** Beat the softened cream cheese and whipped topping together until smooth. Spread over the set gelatin layer.
6. **Garnish & serve:** Top with chopped pecans if desired, slice, and serve chilled!
