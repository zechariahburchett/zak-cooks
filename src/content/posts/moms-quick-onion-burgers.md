## Prep Details

* **Prep time:** 5 mins
* **Cook time:** 15 mins
* **Servings:** 4 servings
* **Method:** Stovetop
* **Cuisine:** American

---

## Ingredients

* 1 lb hamburger (ground beef)
* 1 can Campbell's French Onion Soup (drained)
* 1 tbsp flour
* Water (just enough to mix with flour for a slurry)
* Hamburger buns (for serving)

---

## Instructions

1. **Brown hamburger:** In a skillet over medium heat, brown the 1 lb hamburger. Drain off excess fat.
2. **Add soup & simmer:** Add 1 can of drained Campbell's onion soup and stir to combine. Put a lid on the skillet and let it simmer on medium heat.
3. **Make thickener:** While the mixture simmers, mix 1 tbsp flour with enough water to make a smooth liquid slurry for thickening.
4. **Thicken & finish:** Slowly add the slurry to the hamburger mixture and stir well. Let simmer for about 5 minutes longer until thickened.
5. **Serve:** Spoon warm onto hamburger buns and enjoy!
