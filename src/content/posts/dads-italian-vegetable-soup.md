## Prep Details

* **Prep time:** 15 mins
* **Cook time:** 25 mins
* **Servings:** 6 servings
* **Method:** Stovetop
* **Cuisine:** Italian

---

## Ingredients

* 12 oz cooked link sausage, sliced ¼-inch thick
* 1 med onion, chopped
* 1 can (16 oz) tomato sauce
* 1 can (15 oz) garbanzo beans (with liquid)
* 1 can (10 ½ oz) condensed beef broth
* 1 ½ cups water
* ½ lb zucchini, sliced ¼-inch thick
* 1 bay leaf
* 1 tsp dried basil
* ¼ tsp garlic powder
* ¼ tsp black pepper
* Grated Parmesan cheese (for serving)
* Italian bread (for serving)

---

## Instructions

1. **Sauté sausage and onions:** Cut the sausage into ¼-inch slices. In a large pot over medium heat, sauté the sausage slices with the chopped onion until the onions are softened.
2. **Combine ingredients:** To the same pot, add the tomato sauce, garbanzo beans (with liquid), condensed beef broth, water, sliced zucchini, bay leaf, dried basil, garlic powder, and black pepper.
3. **Simmer:** Bring the soup to a boil. Reduce heat, cover, and simmer until all the vegetables are tender (about 15–20 minutes). Remove the bay leaf before serving.
4. **Serve:** Ladle into bowls, sprinkle grated Parmesan cheese on top, and serve warm with fresh Italian bread!
