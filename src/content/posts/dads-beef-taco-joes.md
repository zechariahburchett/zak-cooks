## Prep Details

* **Prep time:** 5 mins
* **Cook time:** 15 mins
* **Servings:** 4 servings
* **Method:** Stovetop
* **Cuisine:** Tex-Mex

---

## Ingredients

* 1 lb ground beef
* 1 can tomato soup (undiluted)
* 1 cup salsa
* ½ cup cheddar cheese (shredded)

---

## Instructions

1. **Brown the beef:** In a skillet over medium heat, brown the 1 lb ground beef thoroughly. Drain off excess fat.
2. **Heat sauce & combine:** In a separate pan (or directly in the skillet), heat the undiluted tomato soup and salsa. Stir in the browned ground beef until thoroughly combined and heated through.
3. **Melt cheese:** Top with ½ cup cheddar cheese and let it melt over the meat mixture.

---

## Serving Options

Serve warm on rolls, split corn muffins, or spooned over a bed of rice!
