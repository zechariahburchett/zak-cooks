## Prep Details

* **Prep time:** 15 mins
* **Cook time:** 30 mins
* **Servings:** 8–10 servings
* **Method:** Stovetop
* **Cuisine:** American

---

## Ingredients

* 2 lbs ground beef
* 1 lg onion, diced
* 1 to 2 tbsp olive oil (for browning beef)
* 2 cans pork & beans
* 1 lb bacon, cooked and crumbled (keep fat)
* 1 ⅓ cups ketchup (adjust to taste)
* 1 tbsp yellow mustard (adjust to taste)
* 1 ½ cups brown sugar (adjust to taste)
* 2 tbsp reserved bacon fat

---

## Instructions

1. **Brown the meat:** In a large pot or Dutch oven over medium heat, brown the ground beef with the diced onions in 1 to 2 tbsp of olive oil. Drain excess grease.
2. **Add beans & seasonings:** Turn heat down to low/med-low. Add the pork & beans, ketchup, yellow mustard, and brown sugar.
3. **Simmer & add fat:** Bring the mixture to a simmer and stir in the 2 tbsp of reserved bacon fat. Let simmer for 10 to 15 minutes, stirring often.
4. **Add bacon:** Stir in the crumbled cooked bacon and simmer for another 5 to 10 minutes, continuing to stir often.
5. **Serve:** Serve warm right off the stove!
